Arquivo usado no site: `public/images/electrical-hero.webp` (aproximadamente 203 KB). Original preservado em `public/images/electrical-hero-source.png`. Conversão para WebP com Sharp, sem alteração do conteúdo.

Título atual: **Precisou? Chama a Martins.** A composição editorial usa Space Grotesk 500 para a pergunta, Manrope para a frase de ligação e Barlow Condensed 700 para o nome em destaque. Fontes hospedadas localmente e alinhamento óptico entre as linhas. O fundo atual é um feixe de energia em CSS. As fontes são famílias licenciadas; não foi criada uma família exclusiva.

## Prompt usado

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium Brazilian electrician website hero editorial image, portrait 4:5 crop suitable on right half of black and orange layout. Primary request: believable, meticulously composed photography of electrical installation craft with NO PEOPLE. Subject: a professional modern grey residential electrical distribution panel with neatly arranged DIN rail circuit breakers and organized insulated wiring, on a workbench behind a pair of high quality orange-and-black insulated electricians pliers and an insulated screwdriver in the foreground. Panel is completely de-energized, no sparks or live contacts. Only a few believable tools, no clutter. Scene: modest contemporary residential renovation interior with off-white concrete/plaster wall and warm natural window light, not luxury mansion, not a stock-photo office. Composition: cropped close editorial shot with tools resting on charcoal workbench lower right and the breaker panel in middle and upper right, shallow depth of field with panel still readable as electrical equipment, architectural diagonal shadows, strong tactile realism. Medium: exceptionally refined editorial photograph, 50mm lens, naturally detailed metal, rubber, cable insulation and concrete, restrained warm color grade, deep graphite shadows, small orange accents. Lighting: warm side light grazing the tools, realistic soft shadow, plenty of midtone details, sophisticated bright natural background with graphite equipment. Avoid: text, words, logos, humans, workers, faces, hands, excessive orange, glow, sparks, lightning, futuristic gear, neon, diagram, surreal objects, toys, rendered 3D look, fake client project, overly complex exposed hazardous wiring. Create an original high quality bitmap photo-like conceptual illustration. No frame, no UI or typography.
