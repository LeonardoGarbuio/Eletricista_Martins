# Prompts das imagens da vitrine

## lighting

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide 3:2 editorial photograph for an electrician and home maintenance website inspiration gallery. Primary request: exceptionally believable photography of artificial lighting improving a modest contemporary Brazilian living room at dusk. No people. The subject is the lighting design itself: discreet warm white ceiling downlights, an understated linear wall-wash light illuminating a lightly textured off-white wall, one simple pendant lamp, all visibly switched on at comfortable 2700K-3000K. Small inviting living room, approximately 4 by 5 meters, natural wood cabinet, linen grey sofa, calm warm beige wall, clean furnishings, a small plant. Large window shows subdued blue dusk, no daytime sunlight. Style: refined architectural editorial photography, physically realistic, not a luxury mansion, relatable Brazilian residential setting, subtle craft and lived-in plausibility. Composition: horizontal 3:2, architectural eye-level view, subject centered, lower corners simple for interface badges, clearly visible lamp and warm illuminated walls, no clutter. Palette: warm whites, charcoal accents, beige, restrained natural timber, warm light. Realistic soft shadows and natural texture. Avoid: text, logos, watermark, people, hands, sparks, orange-neon strips, science-fiction, exaggerated glow, architectural impossibilities, ultra luxury styling, collage, before-after split. This is an original photorealistic conceptual reference, not a claimed client project.

## electrical

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide 3:2 editorial photograph for a Brazilian electrician website service inspiration gallery. Primary request: a professional, believable residential electrical installation, no people. Subject: a small neat contemporary recessed residential distribution panel, grey cabinet, carefully arranged DIN-rail breakers and tidy insulated cables using realistic blue neutral, green/yellow protective earth and dark phase insulation. Panel is de-energized and photographed as a conceptual still-life installation reference; no sparks, no live contact. One pair of orange and black insulated electricians pliers and one insulated screwdriver rest on a charcoal work surface in the lower foreground. Scene: modest modern Brazilian home renovation, plaster wall, warm directional window light, soft shadows. Style: premium architectural detail/editorial craft photograph, same warm restrained visual family as a residential lighting image, realistic tactile metal and cable insulation, 50mm lens, no sterile stock-photo lab. Composition: horizontal 3:2 photograph, panel centered slightly right, balanced negative space and tangible tools on lower left, the panel and wiring clearly visible, mild depth of field rather than heavy blur, lower image corners not cluttered. Palette: warm plaster white, graphite, subtle orange tool accents, grounded natural colors. Avoid: any humans, faces, hands, text, branding, watermark, ridiculous volumes of wiring, glowing cables, decorative neon, cyberpunk, sparks, cartoon, obvious render, unsafe dramatic work. Original conceptual reference only, not a real company work photograph.

## renovation

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide 3:2 editorial photograph for home renovation and maintenance service inspiration gallery of a Brazilian local company. Primary request: a beautifully finished but accessible modest Brazilian kitchen renovation that shows the value of clean tile work, paint and joinery. No people. Scene: compact modern residential kitchen, off-white matte walls with subtle texture, carefully aligned warm white ceramic backsplash, light natural timber lower cabinets, a simple charcoal countertop and brushed steel faucet, clean tile floor, one restrained small green plant. A neatly folded work cloth and a small paint brush on a protected work surface at one lower corner subtly suggest finishing touches. Everything coherent and realistically buildable, not a mansion, no dramatic demolition, no branded appliances. Style: professional architectural editorial photography, restrained warm daylight matching the tone of electrical and lighting gallery images, natural material realism, soft contrast, 35mm eye-level perspective. Composition: horizontal 3:2, a pleasing oblique view that clearly shows ceramic grout alignment, painted wall and cabinet craftsmanship, no distracting foreground tools, image readable when cropped to 4:3 on mobile. Palette: warm plaster white, grey, natural wood, understated charcoal, calm realism. Avoid: text, logos, watermarks, people, hands, before-and-after split, neon, unrealistic luxury, CGI or video game look, oversaturated orange, incorrect geometry, surreal objects. Original photorealistic conceptual inspiration image, never claimed as the company's completed work.
